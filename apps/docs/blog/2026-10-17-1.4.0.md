This release makes **the edits you make in LibrePhotos reach your files**, lets you **change the date and place of many photos at once**, adds **filters to search**, and brings **album collections and an archive**. Updating gets easier too: the work an update leaves now runs from the app. It also fixes a long list of bugs in scans, dates, faces, places and stacks, and **a security problem**.

**Who should update:** Everyone. If other people have an account on your LibrePhotos, update right away: this release fixes a security problem.

**What's new for you:**

- With **Write edits to files** on, your tags, captions, locations and rotations now go into your files too, next to ratings, dates and face tags. **Write now** under **Write existing edits to files** writes the edits you made before. See [What LibrePhotos writes](/docs/user-guide/exif-data#writing-the-edits-of-a-whole-library).
- Select photos and choose **Change date and time…** or **Set location…** to fix a camera with a wrong clock, or place a whole trip at once. See [Changing the date of many photos](/docs/user-guide/viewing-photos#changing-the-date-of-many-photos).
- Click **Filters** on the search results to narrow them by people, camera, date, place, rating or file type. Search also ignores accents now: "Zurich" finds "Zürich". See [Search filters](/docs/user-guide/search#search-filters).
- Group your albums into **Collections**, and use **Archive** to take photos off the timeline while they stay in your albums. See [Collections](/docs/user-guide/albums#grouping-albums-into-collections) and [Archive](/docs/user-guide/viewing-photos#archive).
- Admins: a **Finish the upgrade** card in the [Admin Area](/docs/user-guide/glossary#admin-area) runs the work an update leaves and shows how far it got. See [The Finish the upgrade card](/docs/installation/upgrading#finish-the-upgrade).

**Before you update:** Make a [backup](/docs/user-guide/glossary#backup) as usual. The [Windows app](/docs/user-guide/glossary#windows-app) and the single container with its built-in database now also copy the database by themselves before every [database update](/docs/user-guide/glossary#database-update). See [Automatic backups](/docs/installation/upgrading#sqlite-backups).

**After you update:**

- **Nothing to do for most of it.** The first start takes longer than usual, because LibrePhotos repairs some data once, for example duplicate Nextcloud photos. Admins see the remaining work on the **Finish the upgrade** card under **Account menu > Admin Area**: it removes photo details from old [thumbnails](/docs/user-guide/glossary#thumbnail) and reads shutter speeds again by itself. The first **Scan** after the update checks every file once, so it takes longer too.
- **Recommended for everyone: [rescan](/docs/user-guide/glossary#rescan) your photos once.** Go to **Account menu > Library**, then the arrow next to **Scan** > **Rescan**. This reads what LibrePhotos did not read before: dates, places and camera details from XMP sidecars, the date of MKV, WebM and phone videos, and Google Takeout files. It is done when the job in **Account menu > My Jobs** has finished. If you skip it, those details only show up for photos that change.
- **Windows app, west of Greenwich (for example in the Americas): the rescan is required.** Up to 1.3.0, the Windows app stored scan times wrongly there, so a normal **Scan** missed files you had edited.
- **Optional: rescan faces once** if face detection ever failed on your server, or if other apps wrote face tags into your photos. Go to **Account menu > Library** and click **Rescan** in the **Rescan Faces** row. If you skip it, faces in those photos stay undetected.
- **If you use stacks:** go to **Photos > Organizing**, open **Stacks** and click **Detect Photo Stacks** once. Bursts are grouped better now, and old stacks stay as they were until you do this.
- **If you write face tags to files:** face tags of rotated photos (most phone portraits) were written to the wrong place. Go to **Account menu > Settings > Metadata Options** and click **Write now** under **Write existing edits to files** to write them again.
- <details><summary>Admins: settings, log file and Docker changes</summary>New site settings: <b>Missing photos limit</b>, <b>Scan libraries automatically</b>, <b>Delete photos from the trash after</b> (off by default) and a <b>Features</b> card. New environment variables: <code>STUCK_JOB_HOURS</code>, <code>SIDECAR_PORT_&lt;NAME&gt;</code>, <code>NEXTCLOUD_CA_BUNDLE</code>, <code>FEATURE_VIDEO_PREVIEWS</code> and the thumbnail and video preview sizes; remove <code>DEFAULT_FAVORITE_MIN_RATING</code> from your <code>.env</code>, it is no longer read (see <a href="https://docs.librephotos.com/docs/installation/environment-variables">Environment variables</a>). The log file is now <code>librephotos.log</code>: point log shippers and fail2ban filters at the new name (<a href="https://docs.librephotos.com/docs/installation/upgrading#log-file-renamed">details</a>). The single container Compose file moved to <code>deploy/compose/docker-compose.unified.yml</code>, reads <code>librephotos.env</code> and mounts the database at <code>/var/lib/postgresql</code>: back up the <code>dbcursor</code> folder in your data folder first. The generated secret key moves to <code>protected_media/secret.key</code>; copy it back before you go back to 1.3.0 or older, or everyone has to log in again (<a href="https://docs.librephotos.com/docs/installation/upgrading#downgrade">details</a>). Scripts that use the API: list endpoints page by 100 with one envelope, errors have one shape, and album ids are numbers everywhere.</details>

**Upstream:**

🚀 LibrePhotos: Your Edits, Written to Your Files

With **Write edits to files** set to sidecar or media file, LibrePhotos now writes your tags (as keywords), captions, locations and rotations back to the photo, like ratings, dates and face tags before. The new **Write existing edits to files** row in **Settings > Metadata Options** writes the edits you made before you turned it on, as a job with progress and Cancel ([#2287](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/2287)). Edits go into the sidecar that already exists (`IMG.jpg.xmp` from digiKam or darktable) instead of a second one, deleting a face also removes its region from the file, and when ExifTool could not write an edit, an orange toast says so instead of a green "done". Reading got the same care: the scan imports captions, title, creator, copyright and more keywords from IPTC, EXIF and XMP, takes dates, GPS, camera and lens from sidecars and from the sidecars of RAW and video variants, reads Google Takeout JSON files, and recognises LibrePhotos' own writes, so a bulk write no longer turns the next scan into a full rescan. The info panel shows camera, lens, exposure, megapixels and coordinates, owners can open an **All metadata** view with every tag of the file and its sidecars, and zip downloads include the XMP sidecars. With writing Off (the default), LibrePhotos now really leaves your files alone: a scan never writes to them.

🚀 LibrePhotos: Edit Many Photos at Once

Select photos and choose **Change date and time…** to shift them all by the same amount (a camera with a wrong clock, or one left on summer time) or give them one date ([#2278](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/2278)), or **Set location…** to place them all at once, optionally copying the position of a photo taken around the same time ([#2279](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/2279)). A photo's location can also be removed now. Changed date rules can be applied to the photos already in the library without a full rescan, captions can be generated for the whole library as one job, the files of selected photos can be rescanned and faces detected for one folder or a selection, and photos can be moved with all their files to another folder of the library. An authenticated scan-files API lets an outside process tell LibrePhotos about new files.

🚀 LibrePhotos: Collections, Archive and Groups of People

Albums can be grouped into **collections** such as Travel or Family, and an album can be in several ([#1009](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/1009)). You can upload straight into one of your albums and create an album before it has photos. The new **Archive** takes photos off the timeline and Memories while they stay in albums, people, places and search ([#545](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/545)), and **Photos not in an album** shows what is left to sort. Album lists sort by name, count or date, come back to the same place on Back, and events can get a cover you pick. People can be put in groups ("Family", "Work") to filter the People page and the face dashboard by, two people can be merged into one, deleted faces can be restored from a new Deleted tab, and the face dashboard gets a Confirm button on each face and a grid of people with their cover faces.

🚀 LibrePhotos: Search Filters

The search results page has a **Filters** button: people (photos with all of them together, [#329](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/329)), camera, lens, ISO, aperture, focal length, rating, date taken, location and file type, including "Has a RAW file" ([#2259](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/2259)). Active filters show as chips you can remove one by one, and they live in the URL, so a link keeps them and Back undoes the last change. You can also search by filters alone, without any words, through **Filter photos** in the command palette. Search now ignores accents and letter case in both directions: `Zurich` finds "Zürich", and `Lodz` finds "Łódź".

🚀 LibrePhotos: Safer Updates and Scans

The work an update leaves is no longer only in the release notes: a **Finish the upgrade** card at the top of the Admin Area runs the follow-up tasks and shows their progress, with a dot on the avatar while anything is left ([#2322](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/2322)). While the backend updates its database or starts, the web app shows **LibrePhotos is updating** instead of empty pages, and a small start-up page answers on the backend port from the first second. The Windows app and the single container with SQLite copy the database before every migration, and an older release refuses to start on a database a newer one has migrated. Scans got safer too: a scan of an unmounted or empty library no longer marks every photo missing, an unreadable folder is skipped instead of failing the whole scan, Cancel really stops a job's work, and a long first scan is no longer failed after 24 hours. Admins can also have LibrePhotos scan every library on a schedule from **Site settings**, with no host cron job ([#235](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/issues/235)).

🚀 LibrePhotos: Security Fix

A logged-in user could change the hash of one of their own photos through the photo API. The hash names the photo's thumbnail files, so rotating the photo afterwards wrote and deleted `.webp` and `.mp4` files outside the thumbnail folders, or replaced another user's thumbnails ([GHSA-f7vw-q3m9-4998](https://github.com/LibrePhotos/librephotos/security/advisories/GHSA-f7vw-q3m9-4998)). The hash is now set by the server only, and every thumbnail path refuses a hash that is not a plain file name. Every API view now also declares who may call it, the token endpoints and failed Basic authentication attempts are rate limited, and the generated secret key is no longer kept in the logs folder. Thanks to [shaoyuuuuu](https://github.com/shaoyuuuuu) for the responsible disclosure.
