# The things OpenCLIP can find in a photo ("Things" albums and search).
#
# One thing per line. A bare word is described to the model with a set of
# prompt templates ("a photo of {word}.", ...). "word = prompt | prompt" uses
# these prompts instead: for words the model reads the wrong way ("glasses",
# "pan") or that need the visual cue spelled out (documents, events).
#
# "## <section> max=<n> min=<p>" starts a section: at most n of its things per
# photo, each with a softmax probability of at least p. The values were chosen
# on 300 photos of a real library (198 to tune, 100 held out), every proposed
# thing graded good, vague or wrong by looking at the photo. On the held-out
# photos they keep as many good things as the old flat 0.75 % cut-off (1.6 per
# photo) with 8x fewer wrong or useless ones (0.9 instead of 6.9 per photo).
# Activities, sports and occasions guessed from loose cues need near
# certainty; people and photo types are reliable at a few percent.
#
# Document detection (api/classification/documents.py) relies on the names of
# the document section: keep them when re-wording their prompts.
