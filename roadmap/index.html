Shipped

The milestones of each release, newest first. The version opens its release notes, the title the part of the docs that explains it.

  1. 1.6.09 Nov 2026
    • Feature
      A fresh look

      A calmer design on every page, also on phones, and big days that load fast without jumping.

    • Feature
      Your year in photos

      Rewind shows your busiest day and the people and places you photographed most.

    • Feature
      Error reports that stay on your server

      Nothing is sent until you choose to share a report.

  2. 1.5.028 Oct 2026
    • Feature
      Share your whole library

      Share everything, one person or one album with another user.

    • Feature
      Photos on the map

      The Places page shows your photos on a map, with places of your own.

    • Feature
      Scroll scrubber styles

      The timeline's scroll bar reaches the header and comes in thumb and density styles.

    • Install
      Updates from the Windows app

      The Windows app downloads a new release and restarts into it.

    • Install
      Any GPU, one image

      Intel, AMD and NVIDIA GPUs run the models in the regular Docker image and the single container.

  3. 1.4.017 Oct 2026
    • Feature
      Your edits, in your files

      Tags, captions, locations and rotations are written back to the photo or its sidecar.

    • Feature
      Search filters

      Narrow a search down by people, camera, exposure, rating, day, location and file.

    • Feature
      Google Takeout imports

      Dates, locations and descriptions from a Google Photos export are read during the scan.

  4. 1.3.011 Oct 2026
    • Feature
      One open model for tags and search

      OpenCLIP finds things on your photos and powers semantic search in a single pass.

    • Feature
      Timeline filter

      Show only photos or only videos, and leave screenshots and documents out of the timeline.

    • Feature
      Videos that play in every browser

      A video your browser cannot play is converted as it plays.

  5. 1.2.029 Sep 2026
    • Install
      A Windows app

      Unzip one download and the whole server runs on Windows, no Docker needed.

    • Feature
      Memories

      The photos you took around this date in earlier years, played back as a slideshow.

    • Feature
      Captions that name people and places

      A small vision-language model writes captions with who is in the photo and where it was taken.

  6. 1.1.028 Aug 2026
    • Feature
      Single sign-on

      Log in with Keycloak, Authentik, Authelia or any other OpenID Connect provider.

    • Feature
      Text search in your photos

      Screenshots and documents get their own category, and the text on them is searchable.

  7. 1.0.026 Jun 2026
    • Install
      Numbered releases

      Releases are numbered instead of named after the week, so updates are easier to follow.

  8. 2026w2521 Jun 2026
    • Feature
      A new face recognition engine

      InsightFace with ArcFace embeddings matches faces more accurately.

  9. 2026w145 Apr 2026
    • Feature
      Face tags written to sidecars

      Named faces are written as standard XMP face regions that other photo apps read.

  10. 2026w091 Mar 2026
    • Feature
      Stacks, file variants and duplicates

      RAW and JPEG pairs and Live Photos become one photo, and duplicates are found for you.

  11. 2025w3528 Aug 2025
    • Install
      A single container

      One Docker image with everything inside, database included.

    • Feature
      Public album links

      Share an album with anyone through a link.

  12. 2023w3715 Sep 2023
    • Install
      A Docker image with GPU support

      Machine learning on NVIDIA GPUs, until 1.5.0 brought every GPU into the regular image.

  13. 2023w181 May 2023
    • Feature
      Motion Photos

      LibrePhotos supports the Motion Photos of Samsung and Google phones.

  14. 2022w4516 Nov 2022
    • Feature
      Dates on the scroll bar

      Scrolling the timeline shows which date you are at.
